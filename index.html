Planned before we arrive

In a hangar we place units along both long sides with the outlets aimed across the guest area. Cold air is heavier than warm air, so it settles low, where people sit, while the sun heats the roof outside.

One team, from first call to dismantling

  1. Site and load check

    Area, structure, open or closed sides, guest count and available power decide how many units go where.

  2. Transport

    Units leave our Ujjain yard on Grand Sameer Events trucks, on the same schedule as the rest of the venue.

  3. Installation

    Indoor and outdoor units are paired, placed along the décor line and tested before guests arrive.

  4. During the event

    Our team stays reachable to adjust settings and handle any unit that needs attention.

  5. Dismantling

    Units are packed, checked against their asset tags and returned to the yard.

Before you book

Based in Ujjain. Working across Madhya Pradesh: Ujjain, Indore, Pithampur, Bhopal and beyond.

How many units will my venue need?

It depends on the area, whether the sides are open or closed, the month and the guest count. Send those on WhatsApp and we will reply with a number of units and a quote.

Who arranges electricity at the venue?

Power at the venue is the client's responsibility. Tell us what supply or generator is planned and we will confirm the load our units need.

Is transport included in the rate?

Transport is quoted separately and depends on the venue location.

How do I confirm a booking?

An advance is needed before installation. Damage to units at the venue is chargeable.

Can we rent for a week or longer?

Yes. Longer rentals are quoted per unit for the period, for sites, offices or facilities that need temporary cooling.

Do you work with event companies?

Yes. Much of our work comes through event companies and tent houses who add cooling to their package.
